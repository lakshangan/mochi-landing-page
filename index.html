    Standing by.

    It watches the app, not your keys.

    Mochi is built to sit next to your work without getting in the way of it.

    • What it reads

      The name of the app in front and how long it has been since you moved the mouse or pressed a key. Mochi never records what you type.

    • Where your things live

      Tasks, notes and settings sit in one file on your computer. There is no account and no telemetry.

    • When it goes online

      Only if you add your own OpenAI key to chat with it. Timers, reminders and commands all work offline.
