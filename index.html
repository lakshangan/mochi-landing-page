It reacts to how you work

Typing makes it bounce. Opening your editor makes it squint at the code. Walk away and it falls asleep, then says welcome back when you return.

Talk to it in plain words

Type “add task review pull request” or “start Pomodoro” and Mochi understands. The command parser runs on your machine, so no internet is needed.

Sized and placed your way

Shrink it to 70 pixels or grow it to 220. Keep it above your windows, let it wander the screen and start it at login.

    Standing by.

    It watches the app, not your keys.

    Mochi is built to sit next to your work without getting in the way of it.

    • What it reads

      The name of the app in front and how long it has been since you moved the mouse or pressed a key. Mochi never records what you type.

    • Where your things live

      Tasks, notes and settings sit in one file on your computer. There is no account and no telemetry.

    • When it goes online

      Only if you add your own OpenAI key to chat with it. Timers, reminders and commands all work offline.
